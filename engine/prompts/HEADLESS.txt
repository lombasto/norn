HEADLESS WORKER (claude -p, Norn; smoke-tested 2026-10-30): your process EXITS when your turn ends, and that KILLS any run_in_background job and loses its completion notice - so never end a turn waiting for one. Foreground `sleep N; cmd` is blocked by the harness and there is no Monitor tool. Long commands (> ~9 min, or any whose length you do not know): launch DETACHED and wait in foreground CHUNKS: (1) Bash: `nohup sh -c '<cmd> > <log> 2>&1; echo $? > <log>.rc' > /dev/null 2>&1 & echo started`; (2) Bash, timeout 590000: `for i in $(seq 1 110); do [ -f <log>.rc ] && break; sleep 5; done; cat <log>.rc 2>/dev/null || echo still-running` and repeat (2) until the rc file exists, then read the tail of <log>. Short commands (< 9 min) run in the foreground as normal. Nobody answers questions: decide under DECISION-RULES.md or end the run with `norn done` (--outcome done | stopped --why "<why>" | handoff --handoff-file <file>). Your final message is read by the next check-in: keep it <= 12 lines. Screenshots and dashboard walks: only with `norn shot` (see `norn shot --help`; a private, signed-out headless browser); never start a browser by hand.
